Fixed Deposit Interest Rates FAQs Can I get monthly interest on FD? Yes, IndusInd bank offers monthly interest payout option and quarterly interest payout option. You can choose any one of the options at the time of booking an FD. Is FD tax-free? As per the Income Tax Act of 1961, FD interest is categorised as 'income from other sources' and is fully taxable. The interest earned on FDs is added to your gross annual income, and taxes are calculated based on the applicable tax laws. When can I receive my FD interest payouts? The payouts based on applicable FD interest rates are typically offered upon maturity, determined at the time of deposit. Some institutions may offer periodic payouts, such as quarterly or annually, depending on the terms agreed upon at the time of creating an FD. Visit IndusInd Bank's FD Calculator to estimate your payouts to make a planned investment. Does IndusInd Bank offer reinvestment of fixed deposits? Yes, IndusInd Bank allows reinvestment of fixed deposits. You can choose from these two reinvestment options at the time of renewing an FD: Renew Principal and Interest Both Renew Principal and Pay Interest Back Does TDS apply to interest earned via an FD? Banks deduct tax at source when the interest credited to your account exceeds Rs. 40,000 for individuals other than senior citizens. For senior citizens, the threshold is Rs. 50,000. This is in accordance with income tax regulations. Does IndusInd Bank offer a tax-saving FD option? Yes, IndusInd Bank does offer a tax-saving FD option known as the Indus Tax Saver Scheme, which provides tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. Can you prematurely withdraw from an FD? Yes, premature withdrawal from an FD is typically allowed, but it may incur penalties or a reduction in interest rates, depending on the bank's terms and conditions. Does IndusInd Bank offer any FDs for senior citizens? Yes, IndusInd Bank does offer special FD schemes for senior citizens, which come with higher interest rates compared to regular FDs. What is the maximum FD limit? The maximum FD limit varies among banks and financial institutions. It depends on factors such as the depositor's eligibility, the bank's policies, and regulatory guidelines. You can check with our bank representatives for the specific maximum FD limit applicable to you. How does the FD interest rate differ from other types of interest rates? FD interest rates are fixed for the entire tenure of the deposit, offering predictability. In contrast, other types of interest rates, such as those for savings accounts or loans, may fluctuate over time based on market conditions or central bank policies. Currently, resident Indian can get up to 6.75% p.a. for 1 year tenure fixed deposit whereas senior citizen customer can get up to7.25% (as of 24 th July’2026). Therefore, for a Rs. 10K deposit, interest earned will be Rs. 692 on maturity whereas for senior Citizens it will be Rs. 745. Note: These calculations are indicative, and interest rates are subject to change. Always check the latest fixed deposit rates before investing.
