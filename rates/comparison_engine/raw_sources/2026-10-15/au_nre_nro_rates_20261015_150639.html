            Terms and Conditions

            1. Retail Fixed Deposits are single Fixed Deposits booked for an amount less than INR 3 Crore w.e.f 8th June 2024.
            2. ** For NRE Fixed Deposit: tenures of 12 months & above are applicable.
            3. Interest rates are subject to periodic changes. The applicable interest rates will be offered based on the date and time of receipt of the funds by the Bank. The depositor shall ascertain the rates on the booking date of Fixed Deposit.
            4. Only resident Indian individual Senior Citizen (of 60 years of age and above) are eligible for the Senior Citizen Interest rate specified. The special rates are applicable only for Resident deposits.
            5. The Bank computes interest based on the actual number of months & days in a year. In case, the deposit is spread over a leap and a non-leap year, the interest is calculated based on the number of days i.e. 366 days in a leap year & 365 days in a non-leap year. The period of Fixed Deposit is calculated in number of months & days.
            6. The interest rate applicable on premature closure of Fixed Deposits (all amounts) will be lower of:

                 (i). the base rate for the original/contracted tenure for which the Fixed Deposit has been booked
                 OR
                 (ii). the base rate applicable for the tenure for which the Fixed Deposit has been in force with the Bank
            The base rate is the rate applicable to Retail Fixed Deposits as on the date of booking the deposit.
            7.In case of NRE deposit no interest will be paid if the NRE Deposit is liquidated before the completion of 1 year from the date of booking.
            8. The penalty on premature closure / withdrawal of Retail Fixed Deposits including sweep-in and partial closures has been fixed by the Bank at the rate of 1%
            9. In case of Domestic Fixed Deposits: No interest will be paid if the deposit is withdrawn within 7 days of the date of booking the deposit
            10. Please read the Terms & Conditions for Fixed Deposits to know more
            11. Interest compounding frequency and interest payout frequency, on our Fixed Deposits, is on anniversary basis
            12. Monthly Payout Fixed Deposits are available only for tenures above 3 months
            13. Monthly Payout Fixed Deposits are available for both individuals (Domestic, NRO, Senior Citizens) and non-individuals
            14. Monthly Payout Fixed Deposits are available at discounted rates as mentioned above
            15. For Interbank Deposit rates contact AU Small Finance Bank Branch
            16. Tax saver Fixed deposits can only be booked for a tenure of 60 months and interest rates as mentioned above for the tenure shall apply.
            17. AU Small Finance Bank also offers MIBOR linked Term Deposits. For more details, please contact your Relationship Manager or visit your nearest AU Small Finance Bank branch.
