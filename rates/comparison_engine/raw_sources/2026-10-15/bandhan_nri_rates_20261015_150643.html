Note:

  • The above rates are applicable only for term deposits less than ₹3 crore.
  • Senior Citizen Rates are only for Resident Indians and do not apply to NRIs.
  • Submission of proof of Age is mandatory to avail Senior Citizen Rates.
  • Under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. 1961, a maximum tax exemption up to ₹1,50,000 may be availed by investing in Bandhan Bank Tax Saver Fixed Deposits.
  • Tax Saver Fixed Deposits cannot be closed prematurely/partially before expiry of the lock-in period of 5 years and cannot be booked with auto renewal facility.
  • TDS is applicable for Interest earned over ₹50,000 for general customers and ₹1,00,000 for Sr. Citizen Customers in a year.
  • Customer can submit Form 121 as applicable if the annual income is below ₹4,00,000 for general and ₹12,00,000 for Senior citizen customers. Additionally, Form 121 has to be submitted by customers for any new retail term deposit (RTD) booking during the Financial Year.
  • Tax Saver Fixed deposits are non-market-linked investments, so they carry zero risk and are safe investments.
  • Tax Saver Fixed deposits are applicable for Resident Indians, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs) and NRO customers.
  • Minimum amount of Tax Saver deposits that can be made is ₹1,000.
  • Interest earned on the Fixed Deposits will be subject to Tax Deducted at Source as per extant Indian Income Tax laws.
  • Submission of Income Tax PAN No. is mandatory, failing which TDS will be deducted at a higher rate as per Indian Income Tax laws.
  • Interest rates are subject to change from time to time. Applicable interest rates will be given as on the date / time of receipt of the funds by the Bank.
  • In case of Premature withdrawal, penal interest rate of 1%, as decided by the competent authority, shall be applicable on the card rate. Interest shall be paid at the rate applicable to the amount withdrawn and period for which the amount remained with the bank and not at the contracted rate.
  • Minimum tenure for Domestic & NRO term deposits is 7 days and no interest is payable for deposits prematurely withdrawn within the period of 7 days from the date of deposit.
  • Minimum tenure for NRE term deposits is 1 year and no interest is payable for deposits prematurely withdrawn within the period of 1 year from the date of deposit.
  • In case of reinvestment deposits, the interest reinvested is post TDS recovery and hence the maturity amount for re-investment deposits would vary to the extent of tax and compounding effect on tax for the period subsequent of deduction till maturity.
  • Interest rates are subject to change without any prior notice.
